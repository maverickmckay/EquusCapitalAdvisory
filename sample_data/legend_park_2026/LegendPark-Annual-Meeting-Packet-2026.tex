\section*{Packet Overview}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Packet Overview}

Annual Meeting of the Legend Park Condominium Association. It follows the structure of prior
Legend Park annual meeting packets and incorporates the Association's approved budgets and
financial statements for fiscal years 2025 and 2026, including the 2025 year-end financials
and 2026 year-to-date results through April 30, 2026.

\section{2025 Annual Meeting Minutes}

\subsection*{Minutes of the 2025 Annual Meeting of the Members}
\vspace{-0.6em}

\noindent
\textbf{Legend Park Condominium Association}\\
\textbf{Annual Meeting of the Members}\\
\textbf{2025 Annual Meeting -- 6{:}00 p.m.}\\
Hales Corners Library -- Hunt Room

\vspace{1em}

\noindent
\strong{1. Call to Order}\\[0.5em]
The meeting was called to order at 6{:}06 p.m.

\vspace{0.75em}

\noindent
\strong{2. Determination of Quorum}\\[0.5em]
Quorum was \textbf{not met}. Despite the absence of a quorum sufficient to conduct formal
voting, informational items, maintenance updates, and board status were reviewed for the
benefit of owners in attendance. No formal motions could be voted upon.

\vspace{0.75em}

\noindent
\strong{3. Introductions}\\[0.5em]
The following Board members and management representative introduced themselves to the
owners in attendance:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.1em]
    \item \strong{Jenny Laatsch} --- President
    \item \strong{Steve Roth} --- Secretary
    \item \strong{Kristen Yohpe} --- Vice President
    \item \strong{Becky Mainus} --- Resident at Large
    \item \strong{Ruth Hozeska} --- Not in attendance
    \item \strong{Paige} --- Mike \& Mike's Management (formerly PMI)
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.75em}

\noindent
\strong{4. Approval of 2024 Annual Meeting Minutes}\\[0.5em]
The 2024 Annual Meeting Minutes were reviewed and approved by those in attendance.

\vspace{0.75em}

\noindent
\strong{5. 2024 Financial Report}\\[0.5em]
The 2024 financial report was presented and discussed with owners in attendance.

\vspace{0.75em}

\noindent
\strong{6. President's Report}\\[0.5em]
The President provided a detailed update on the Association's ongoing capital and maintenance
priorities:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.1em]
    \item \strong{Roofing:} Work on the last building has been completed. All roofing projects
    identified in the prior capital plan are now finished.

    \item \strong{Driveways:} The Board reviewed options for driveway surfaces. The decision
    was made to prioritize replacement and targeted spot repairs rather than sealing, given
    the condition of the existing surfaces and long-term cost considerations.

    \item \strong{Painting:} Painting projects for wood and stucco surfaces have been approved.
    The work covers exterior components identified in the maintenance review, with scheduling
    to be confirmed with the selected contractor.

    \item \strong{Masonry:} Tuck-pointing work has been approved and scheduled. Masonry
    deterioration in several locations was identified as a priority repair item.

    \item \strong{Mulch and Landscaping:} Mulch installation and general landscaping work was
    deferred from the current season due to the higher priority of maintenance and structural
    repair items. This work is expected to be addressed in the following season.

    \item \strong{Retaining Walls:} Retaining wall repair and potential replacement needs were
    identified. The Board is gathering information on scope and cost for future planning.

    \item \strong{Water Concerns:} Specific units have experienced water infiltration issues.
    The Board is tracking these situations and working to identify and resolve the source of
    each occurrence.

    \item \strong{Snow Removal:} Performance from the prior snow removal vendor was discussed,
    with the Board acknowledging concerns about service levels and assessing vendor options
    going forward.

    \item \strong{Gutters:} The importance of maintaining clean gutters as a preventive
    maintenance measure was emphasized. Gutter cleaning is planned for both spring and fall
    going forward.

    \item \strong{Seeding and Weed Control:} The Board discussed plans for seeding in areas
    affected by municipal utility work, including coordination with the City on any restoration
    obligations.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.75em}

\noindent
\strong{7. Spring Walk-Around Maintenance Review}\\[0.5em]
Items identified during the spring property walk-around were reviewed. The walk-around
findings were consistent with the maintenance priorities described in the President's Report
and will be incorporated into the contractor scheduling for the current and upcoming season.

\vspace{0.75em}

\noindent
\strong{8. Old Business}\\[0.5em]
No formal motions were made under Old Business given the absence of quorum. Ongoing
maintenance items and outstanding contractor coordination were noted for Board follow-up.

\vspace{0.75em}

\noindent
\strong{9. New Business}\\[0.5em]
The Board and owners in attendance discussed several unit-specific issues including:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.1em]
    \item Location and operation of exterior water shutoffs for individual units.
    \item Exterior conditions around window wells and appropriate drainage measures to
    reduce risk of basement water infiltration.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.75em}

\noindent
\strong{10. Board Nominations -- 2 Open Seats}\\[0.5em]
Two Board seats were announced as open for the 2025 election. No nominations were received
from the floor or in advance of the meeting.

\vspace{0.75em}

\noindent
\strong{11. Board Election}\\[0.5em]
With no nominees presented, no election was held. All current Board members remained in
their positions. The Board noted that the open seats will continue to be available should
qualified owners come forward to serve.

\vspace{0.75em}

\noindent
\strong{12. Adjournment}\\[0.5em]
The meeting was adjourned following the completion of the above agenda items.

\vspace{1.5em}

\noindent
\textit{Note for 2026:} Because quorum was not achieved at the 2025 Annual Meeting, no
formal binding votes were taken. These minutes are presented as an informational record of
the discussions held. The 2026 meeting chair should note this status when addressing the
prior minutes item on the agenda and record it in the 2026 minutes.
