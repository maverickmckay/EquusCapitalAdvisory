BYLAWS OF LAKESIDE CONDOMINIUM ASSOCIATION, INC.
A Wisconsin Nonprofit Corporation

ARTICLE I — NAME AND PURPOSE

Section 1.1 Name. The name of this association is Lakeside Condominium Association, Inc.
(the "Association"), a Wisconsin nonprofit corporation.

Section 1.2 Purpose. The Association is organized to provide for the maintenance,
management, and operation of the common elements of Lakeside Condominiums, a
Wisconsin condominium located in Dane County, Wisconsin, and to promote the health,
safety, and welfare of the unit owners.

ARTICLE II — DEFINITIONS

As used in these Bylaws, the following terms have the following meanings:
"Declaration" means the Declaration of Condominium for Lakeside Condominiums recorded
in the office of the Register of Deeds for Dane County, Wisconsin.
"Unit Owner" means the person or persons holding fee simple title to a unit in the condominium.
"Common Elements" means those portions of the condominium property other than the units.
"Association" means Lakeside Condominium Association, Inc.

ARTICLE III — MEETINGS OF UNIT OWNERS

Section 3.1 Annual Meeting. An annual meeting of unit owners of the Association shall
be held each year for the purpose of electing directors and for the transaction of other
business properly brought before the meeting. The annual meeting shall be held at a
location within Dane County, Wisconsin, as designated by the Board of Directors.

Section 3.2 Notice. Written notice of each meeting of unit owners stating the time, date,
and place of the meeting shall be given by the Secretary (or other authorized officer or
management agent) to each unit owner of record not less than fifteen (15) days nor more
than sixty (60) days before the meeting. Notice shall be delivered personally, by
United States mail, or by electronic mail to the unit owner at the address furnished by
the unit owner to the Association.

Section 3.3 Quorum. Owners entitled to cast twenty-five percent (25%) of the total votes
of the Association, represented in person or by proxy, shall constitute a quorum for the
transaction of business at any annual or special meeting of unit owners. If a quorum is
not present at any meeting of unit owners, the meeting may be adjourned by a majority
vote of those present, in person or by proxy. At any adjourned meeting at which a quorum
is present, any business may be transacted that might have been transacted at the original
meeting.

Section 3.4 Voting. Each unit owner shall be entitled to one (1) vote per unit owned.
A unit owned by more than one person shall have only one vote. Where two or more
persons are entitled to cast one vote, such vote may be cast only by one of them.
Fractional votes shall not be permitted.

Section 3.5 Proxies. A unit owner may vote by proxy. Every proxy shall be in writing and
signed by the unit owner. No proxy shall be valid after eleven (11) months from its date
unless the proxy expressly provides for a longer period, but no proxy shall be valid after
three (3) years from its date. A proxy shall be effective only for the specific meeting
for which it is given and any adjournment of that meeting. A proxy may be revoked by the
unit owner at any time prior to the exercise of the authority granted by the proxy.

Section 3.6 Order of Business. The order of business at the annual meeting of unit owners
shall include the following items: (1) Call to Order; (2) Proof of Notice; (3) Establishment
of Quorum; (4) Approval of Minutes of Prior Annual Meeting; (5) Election of Directors;
(6) Financial Report; (7) Old Business; (8) New Business; (9) Adjournment.

ARTICLE IV — BOARD OF DIRECTORS

Section 4.1 Number and Qualification. The business and affairs of the Association shall
be managed by its Board of Directors. The Board of Directors shall consist of five (5)
directors. Each director shall be a unit owner or an officer, director, partner, trustee,
member, or manager of an entity that is a unit owner, and shall be in good standing with
the Association (not in default in payment of any assessment or charge).

Section 4.2 Term of Office. Directors shall serve three-year (3-year) terms. The terms
of directors shall be staggered so that the terms of not more than two (2) directors expire
in any single year, as set forth in Exhibit A to these Bylaws. Directors shall hold office
until their successors have been elected and qualified.

Section 4.3 Election of Directors. Directors shall be elected by the unit owners at each
annual meeting. Nominations may be made by a Nominating Committee appointed by the Board
prior to the meeting, or by any unit owner from the floor at the annual meeting.
Election shall be by written ballot if there are more candidates than seats to be filled,
or by voice vote or show of hands if the number of candidates equals the number of
seats to be filled.

Section 4.4 Organizational Meeting. Immediately following the annual meeting of unit owners,
the Board of Directors shall hold an organizational meeting for the purpose of electing
officers and conducting other necessary business. No advance notice of the organizational
meeting is required.

Section 4.5 Vacancies. Any vacancy on the Board of Directors shall be filled by a vote
of a majority of the remaining directors. The director appointed to fill a vacancy shall
serve for the unexpired term of the director whose seat was vacated.

ARTICLE V — OFFICERS

Section 5.1 Officers. The officers of the Association shall be a President, a Vice President,
a Secretary, and a Treasurer. The officers shall be elected by the Board of Directors at
the organizational meeting of the Board held immediately following each annual meeting
of unit owners.

ARTICLE VI — FINANCIAL MATTERS

Section 6.1 Budget. The Board of Directors shall prepare or cause to be prepared an annual
operating budget for each fiscal year. A summary of the annual budget shall be distributed
to each unit owner within thirty (30) days after its adoption by the Board.

Section 6.2 Annual Accounting. The Board of Directors shall provide each unit owner with
a written itemized annual accounting of all receipts and expenditures of the Association
within ninety (90) days after the close of the fiscal year, or at the annual meeting of
unit owners, whichever is earlier.

Section 6.3 Fiscal Year. The fiscal year of the Association shall end on December 31
of each year.

ARTICLE VII — AMENDMENTS

Section 7.1 Amendment of Bylaws. These Bylaws may be amended by a vote of unit owners
entitled to cast not less than two-thirds (2/3) of the total votes of the Association
at any annual or special meeting of unit owners, provided that the text of any proposed
amendment shall be included in the notice of the meeting or distributed to all unit owners
at least fifteen (15) days prior to the meeting.

CERTIFICATION

The foregoing Bylaws were adopted by the unit owners of Lakeside Condominium Association, Inc.
at the organizational meeting of unit owners held on January 15, 2010, and are hereby
certified as the current bylaws of the Association.
